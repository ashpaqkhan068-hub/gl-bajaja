The Multi-Round Price Negotiation System

Unlike static price websites or opaque bidding systems, KaamNearby implements a formal, state-tracked negotiation process:

Example Workflow:
  1. Customer submits a booking request offering ₹400 for a ceiling fan installation.
  2. The electrician inspects the job description and counters with ₹500 with a note: "Includes heavy-duty safety bracket assembly."
  3. The customer reviews the counter-offer and proposes ₹450.
  4. The electrician clicks "Accept Offer". The booking is instantly confirmed at ₹450!

Both parties have full visibility of the chronological offer log. Once an offer is accepted by either party, the agreed price is locked, preventing unexpected charges at the doorstep.

For Workers: Getting Verified & Hired

To maintain safety and quality across KaamNearby:

  • Document Submission: Upload government photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, or ITI Trade Certificate).
  • Admin Review: Our administrative team verifies document credentials before issuing the public "Verified" badge.
  • Availability Toggle: Go offline whenever you are busy, and go online to accept new local requests.
