Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

How does price negotiation work?

When you request a service, you submit an initial price offer. The worker can accept your offer or submit a counter-offer with an explanatory note. You can then accept the counter-offer or propose another figure. The booking is only confirmed once both sides agree on a final amount.

How are workers verified?

Every worker submits government-issued identification (such as Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Trade Certification). Our administrators manually inspect the documentation before issuing a "Verified" badge.

When do I pay for the service?

You only pay the worker the mutually agreed price once the job has been completed to your satisfaction at your residence or workspace.
