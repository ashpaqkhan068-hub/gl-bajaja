Our Mission & Story

Empowering Local Blue-Collar Services

KaamNearby connects households with verified local tradespeople, ensuring fair wages, transparent pricing, and trusted service.

The Problem We Solve

Every day, millions of households struggle to find dependable electricians, plumbers, painters, and carpenters when repairs are needed. Simultaneously, highly skilled local workers face unpredictable work, lack digital presence, and are frequently squeezed by intermediaries taking steep commissions.

The KaamNearby Difference

KaamNearby was built on three foundational pillars:

  • Government Identity Verification: Every worker is vetted using government identification (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, or ITI technical certification) verified by our team.
  • Transparent Price Negotiation: Instead of arbitrary algorithmic surge pricing, customers and workers negotiate directly on a clean multi-round counter-offer timeline.
  • Worker Dignity & Direct Earnings: Workers retain control over their starting prices, availability, and scheduling without hidden middleman fees.

Our Vision

To build India's most trustworthy, hyperlocal network for on-demand home services, turning everyday craftsmanship into respected, stable livelihoods.
