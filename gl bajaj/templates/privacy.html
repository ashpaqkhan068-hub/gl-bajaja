Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect registration details (name, email, phone number, city) and worker professional profiles (trade category, experience, starting prices). For verification, workers securely upload identification documents.

2. How We Protect Your Data

Uploaded verification documents (such as Aadhaar, PAN, and voter IDs) are strictly restricted to administrative review and never exposed publicly on the internet or search engines. Passwords are encrypted using irreversible cryptographic hashing algorithms.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data to advertisers. Contact details (phone number and address) are shared only between customer and worker upon a confirmed booking to facilitate service delivery.

4. Your Rights

Users may access and update their profile details at any time through their account dashboard.
